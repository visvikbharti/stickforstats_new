## DATA ANALYSIS NOTES

### Key Variables to Track:

**Background**:
- Role (student vs professional)
- Field
- Years of experience

**Statistical Experience**:
- Self-rated experience level (1-5)
- Number of courses
- Software used (count)
- Frequency of use

**Topic Familiarity**:
- PCA familiarity (1-5)
- CI familiarity (1-5)
- DOE familiarity (1-5)

**Learning Preferences**:
- Preferred resources (multiple)
- Preferred learning method (single)
- Importance of visualizations (1-5)

**Attitudes**:
- Feelings about statistics (categorical)
- Biggest challenges (top 2)

### Analysis Plan:

1. **Demographic Summary**: Frequencies and percentages for role, field, experience
2. **Experience Metrics**: Mean and SD for experience ratings
3. **Software Usage**: Count of software tools, modal primary software
4. **Topic Familiarity**: Mean familiarity scores for PCA, CI, DOE
5. **Learning Preferences**: Mode for preferred method, frequency distribution for resources
6. **Correlations**: Experience level × satisfaction ratings (from post-survey)
