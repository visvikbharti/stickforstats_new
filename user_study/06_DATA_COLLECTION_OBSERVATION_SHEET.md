## APPENDIX: QUICK REFERENCE CODES

### Engagement Levels:
1 = Completely disengaged (distracted, not paying attention)
2 = Minimally engaged (doing it, but without interest)
3 = Moderately engaged (focused, but neutral)
4 = Highly engaged (interested, focused, asking questions mentally)
5 = Extremely engaged (excited, making connections, "aha moments")

### Confusion Severity:
1 = Minor confusion (brief pause, figured it out quickly)
2 = Noticeable confusion (hesitation, re-reading)
3 = Moderate confusion (stuck for 30-60 sec, needed to backtrack)
4 = Significant confusion (stuck for 1-2 min, verbally expressed confusion)
5 = Critical confusion (couldn't proceed without intervention)

### Help Intervention Types:
- **Task clarification**: "Just to clarify, you're completing Lesson 1..."
- **Think-aloud reminder**: "Remember to tell me what you're thinking"
- **Technical help**: Fixed a platform bug or connectivity issue
- **Encouragement**: "You're doing great, keep going"
- **Permission to move on**: "It's okay to skip this if you're stuck"
