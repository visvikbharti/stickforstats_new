## PURPOSE OF THE STUDY

You are being asked to participate in a research study that evaluates the usability and educational effectiveness of an integrated learning system in statistical software. The study aims to understand how users interact with interactive lessons embedded in professional analysis tools.

This research will contribute to the design of educational technology for statistical software and may be published in academic journals or presented at conferences.

---

## PROCEDURES

If you agree to participate, you will be asked to:

1. **Complete a brief pre-study survey** (~5 minutes) about your background and experience with statistics

2. **Participate in one remote session** (90-120 minutes) where you will:
   - Complete 3 interactive lessons on statistical topics (Principal Component Analysis and Confidence Intervals) using a web-based platform
   - "Think aloud" while working, sharing your thoughts and reactions
   - Allow the researcher to observe your screen and take notes

3. **Complete a post-study survey** (~10 minutes) about your experience and satisfaction with the platform

4. **Optional brief interview** (~5 minutes) where you can share additional feedback

**Total Time Commitment**: Approximately 2 hours

Your participation in this study is **completely voluntary**. You may:

## CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

Your privacy will be protected through the following measures:

### Data Collection:
- You will be assigned a participant ID (e.g., P01, P02)
- Your name will not be associated with your responses in the research dataset
- Demographic information (role, experience level) will be reported in aggregate only

### Data Storage:
- All data will be stored on password-protected computers
- Only the research team will have access to identifiable information
- Published results will use anonymized, aggregated data
- Recordings and identifiable data will be destroyed [5 years after study completion]

### Data Use:
- Results may be published in academic journals or presented at conferences
- No personally identifiable information will be published
- Direct quotes (from qualitative feedback) may be used, but will not include identifying information

### Limitations:
- **Federal regulations** may require inspection of research records by government agencies or institutional review boards
- **Research team** members will have access to data for analysis
- **Mandatory reporting**: If evidence of child abuse, elder abuse, or intent to harm self/others emerges, we are legally required to report

## NOTES FOR RESEARCHER

### Obtaining Informed Consent (Before Session Starts):

**Script**:
"Before we begin, I need to review the consent form with you. I've sent you a copy - do you have it in front of you? [Wait for confirmation]

This study is about evaluating educational features in statistical software. You'll complete some interactive lessons and provide feedback. The whole session will take about 90-120 minutes.

Your participation is completely voluntary. You can stop at any time, skip any question, and there are no wrong answers.

With your permission, I'd like to record the session for analysis. The recording will only be seen by the research team and will be kept confidential. Your name won't be associated with it. Is it okay if I record? [Get verbal consent]

Do you have any questions about the study or the consent form? [Answer questions]

If you're ready to proceed, please type your name in the consent form as your electronic signature, and send it back to me. [Or: If you've already signed, we're all set!]

Great! Let's get started."

### When to Stop the Session:

- If participant expresses discomfort or stress
- If participant explicitly requests to stop
- If technical issues make the session impossible to continue
- If participant appears fatigued or frustrated beyond acceptable levels

Always remind them they can take breaks or withdraw without penalty.
