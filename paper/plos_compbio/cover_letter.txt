Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit "StickForStats: Automated Statistical Assumption Validation for Reproducible Computational Biology" for consideration as a Technical Note in GigaScience.

Statistical assumption violations are a pervasive and under-addressed contributor to irreproducibility in biomedical research, yet no widely adopted platform enforces assumption checking automatically. StickForStats closes this gap through the Guardian system — a middleware pipeline of eight validators that intercepts every statistical test, checks its assumptions before execution, and either proceeds with a transparent confidence report or cascades to an appropriate nonparametric alternative with a documented decision trail. A complementary module extends the same validator infrastructure to published manuscripts, checking statistical claims against CONSORT, STROBE, ICH-E9, and JARS-Quant reporting standards.

We believe the work is a natural fit for GigaScience's focus on reproducibility and openly available research objects:
- The platform is fully open-source (MIT license), containerized, and released at a citable version (v1.0.0).
- We demonstrate Guardian on four real datasets — a CRISPR editing-strategy comparison (CRISPRArchitect v3 scores), a UCI Wine Quality correlation, a sixteen-trial meta-analysis of intravenous magnesium for acute myocardial infarction (metafor::dat.egger2001), and a genome-scale RNA-seq differential-expression analysis (GEO GSE271517).
- All reported values are reproducible from the scripts in the repository's replication directory, validated against SciPy and R, and the source code and curated datasets will be deposited in GigaDB upon acceptance.

The software comprises more than 1,500 automated tests under continuous integration, with all calculations cross-validated against SciPy and R.

Use of AI-assisted technologies: generative AI (Claude, Anthropic) was used to assist software development and manuscript drafting; all code and text were reviewed and verified by the authors, every statistical value was independently recomputed, and the authors take full responsibility for the content. No AI tool is listed as an author.
