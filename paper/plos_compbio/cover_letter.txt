Dear Editors,

We submit "StickForStats: Automated Statistical Assumption Validation for Reproducible Computational Biology" as a Software article for PLOS Computational Biology.

Statistical assumption violations are pervasive in published biomedical research, yet no widely adopted platform enforces assumption checking automatically. StickForStats addresses this gap through the Guardian system — a middleware pipeline of eight validators that intercept every statistical test, check assumptions, and either proceed with confidence reporting or cascade to an appropriate nonparametric alternative.

The platform is particularly relevant to computational biology:
- We demonstrate Guardian on real genome editing strategy scores from CRISPRArchitect v3, where it detects normality violations and cascades from ANOVA to Kruskal-Wallis
- A genomics differential expression module performs per-gene Guardian validation with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction
- Clinical trial manuscript review validates against CONSORT and ICH-E9 guidelines
- Five curated biological example datasets are included

The software is open source (MIT license), with 1,088 automated tests, continuous integration, and all calculations validated against SciPy and R to 14-16 decimal places.
