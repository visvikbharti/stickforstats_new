## 6. Known Intentional Items (Not Bugs)

These were flagged during audit but confirmed as correct:
- Canvas 2D drawing colors — intentionally kept for rendering
- Recharts/chart data colors — intentionally kept for visualization
- Export backgrounds — intentionally white for print
- KS test `np.std()` without ddof — correct (MLE expects population SD)
- `1-exp()` in statisticalDistributions.js — Anderson-Darling p-value formula (D'Agostino & Stephens)
- `1-exp()` in ProbabilityCalculator.jsx — exponential/Weibull CDF formulas
- `1-exp()` in AlphaSpendingCalculator.jsx — Hwang-Shih-DeCani spending function
- Cohen's d = 0.494 reported as 0.50 in paper — trivial rounding, not corrected
