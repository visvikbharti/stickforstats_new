!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Human iPSC culture A. Matrigel-coated plates. 1. Thaw a tube of growth factor-reduced Matrigel on ice (Corning, # 354230) on ice 2. Add 120 l Matrigel to 12ml of DMEM/F12 (Thermo Fisher # 12500096) at 4°C (enough for 1-2 plates) and put it in ice. 3. Plate 0.5 ml mix per well in two 12-well plates (Falcon corning, # 353043, or equivalent). 4. Place plates in 37oC incubator for at least 30 min. Plates may be kept for up to 2 weeks in 37°C incubator without drying out. B. Thawing and initial plating of iPSCs. 1. iPSCs were generated by the California Institute of Regenerative Medicine and purchased from the Coriell Institute (now managed by FUJIFILM Cellular Dynamics). 2. Thaw frozen iPS vial in 37°C water bath until just thawed. Add thawed cells to 5 mL mTeSR Plus medium (Stemcell technologies, Catalog # 100-0276) in a 15 ml conical tube (Falcon corning, # 352097). Spin and wash cells with ~1 mL of mTeSR Plus medium. Spin cells and resuspend in 2 ml of mTeSR Plus medium containing 10 uM Y2763 ROCK inhibitor (Tocris, #1254).  Transfer 1 ml of cells each into two wells of the Matrigel coated 12 well plate. Place into hypoxic (5% O2) incubator. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor). C. Passage of iPSCs by the clump culture method. 1. Ideally, cells should be growing in small clumps and reached 65–80% confluence before passage. Split at 1:3 to 1:5 every 3–4 days to avoid confluence which can lead to spontaneous loss of pluripotency. 2. Rinse cells w/ PBS, add 300 l Accutase (Gibco, # A1110501), and incubate for 5 min at 37oC. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. Dilute cells as needed in mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor and plate cells into 3-5 wells of a Matrigel coated 12-well plate. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor) D. Cryopreservation of iPSCs. 1. Rinse cells w/ PBS, add 300 l Accutase, and incubate for 5 min at 37oC. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. 2. Spin down the cells, resuspend w/ 1ml mFreSR medium (Stemcell, # 05855) containing 10 uM Rock inhibitor. 3. Transfer 1 ml cells to cryovial. Transfer vial to a slow freezing container and store at -80oC overnight, then transfer the frozen tubes to liquid nitrogen tank for long term storage. Phase 1: Embryoid body formation A. Growth of iPSCs to not more than 50% confluence on a Matrigel-coated P100 TC dish. 1. When iPSCs reach 65–80% confluence in a well of a 12 well plate, detach iPSC with Accutase as above, but pipet cells thoroughly to obtain single cell suspension. 2. Count cells and plate 7 x 10^5 cells onto a Matrigel coated P100 plate (Corning Falcon, # 353003) as described above. 3. Change to mTeSR+ medium w/o Rock inhibitor the next day. 4. About 2 days later the iPSC should not higher than 50% confluent.  Keeping the cells at this density is a critical step for successful iPSM differentiation. B. Formation of embryoid bodies in AggreWell microwell plate 1. Pre-treat one well for each iPS line in a 24-well AggreWell 800 plate (each well has 300 micropits) with 500 L Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution (both contained in Stemcell Technologies, catalog#: 34850). 2. Centrifuge plate at 1300 x g for 5 minutes in a swinging bucket rotor fitted with plate holders. Observe plate under a microscope to ensure that bubbles have been removed from microwells. If bubbles remain trapped in any microwells, centrifuge at 1300 x g for an additional 5 minutes. 3. Aspirate Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution from the wells. Rinse each well with 2 ml warm mTeSR Plus medium. 4. Aspirate medium from the well. Add 1 ml warm EB medium (Table 1) supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor to each well to be used. 5. Rinse 50% confluent iPSC w/ PBS and detach iPSCs with Accutase, as described above, but pipet thoroughly into single cells. Then add mTeSR Plus to quench Accutase, as above. 6. Count cells, spin down 4.5 x 10^6 IPSCs at 250 x g for 5 min. Resuspend pellet in 1 ml of EB medium supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor (10 M). 7. Add cells to well to yield achieve a final volume of: 2 mL. 8. Pipette cells up and down gently several times with P1000 pipettor to ensure even distribution. Spin plate at 100 x g for 3min. Under microscope, piled iPSCs should be observed in each microwell (Figures 1, 2). 9. Incubate the plate at 37°C in 5% O2 5% CO2 incubator. Feed with 1 ml EB medium (w/o rock inhibitor) every day for 7 days. Compact EB should be observed by day 2 (Figure 2). Phase 2: Generation of macrophage progenitors. A. Add 10 ml macrophage progenitor culture medium (Table 2) to gelatin-coated (4 ml of 0.1% gelatin incubated at 37oC for 1 hr, MP, catalog number: 901771) P100 tissue culture dish. B. Remove and discard 0.5 ml media from each well of the EB culture, yielding 1.5 ml remaining. Cut a P1000 pipet tip with sterile blade to widen the opening. Gently suspend EBs with P1000 pipet tip to dislocate each EB from the microwells. C. Transfer free EBs to a 1.5 ml sterile microfuge tube. EBs will spontaneously sediment in 1 min. Discard the supernatant and use wide open P1000 pipet tip to resuspend the EBs with progenitor culture medium.  D. Transfer resuspended EBs into previously prepared P100 tissue culture dish mentioned in step A. E. Move the P100 dish back and forth and side to side, without rotating, to evenly distribute EBs and incubate in hypoxic incubator for the first 7 days of phase 2, then move to regular incubator. Replenish with progenitor culture medium every 4 days. EBs will attach to the P100 dish and began to grow out. Harvesting macrophage progenitors. Over time, macrophage progenitors are released from the EBs and float in medium. Starting on day 29, floating progenitors in 10 ml medium may be harvested every 4 days, which are then passed through a sterile 50 m cell filter (Sysmex, #04-004-2326), to remove EB fragments, and plated onto a new gelatin coated P100 tissue culture plate. The phase 2 EBs are replenished with 10 ml progenitor culture medium. Phase 3: iPSMs maturation and maintenance Macrophage progenitors attach to the gelatin coated plates overnight and the medium is replaced with macrophage maintenance medium (Table 3).  Replenish cells with fresh maintenance medium every 4 days until usage (usually 7-10 days after harvesting progenitors).

Plate 0.5 ml mix per well in two 12-well plates (Falcon corning, # 353043, or equivalent). 4. Place plates in 37oC incubator for at least 30 min. Plates may be kept for up to 2 weeks in 37°C incubator without drying out. B. Thawing and initial plating of iPSCs. 1. iPSCs were generated by the California Institute of Regenerative Medicine and purchased from the Coriell Institute (now managed by FUJIFILM Cellular Dynamics). 2. Thaw frozen iPS vial in 37°C water bath until just thawed. Add thawed cells to 5 mL mTeSR Plus medium (Stemcell technologies, Catalog # 100-0276) in a 15 ml conical tube (Falcon corning, # 352097). Spin and wash cells with ~1 mL of mTeSR Plus medium. Spin cells and resuspend in 2 ml of mTeSR Plus medium containing 10 uM Y2763 ROCK inhibitor (Tocris, #1254).  Transfer 1 ml of cells each into two wells of the Matrigel coated 12 well plate. Place into hypoxic (5% O2) incubator. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor). C. Passage of iPSCs by the clump culture method. 1. Ideally, cells should be growing in small clumps and reached 65–80% confluence before passage. Split at 1:3 to 1:5 every 3–4 days to avoid confluence which can lead to spontaneous loss of pluripotency. 2. Rinse cells w/ PBS, add 300 l Accutase (Gibco, # A1110501), and incubate for 5 min at 37oC. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. Dilute cells as needed in mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor and plate cells into 3-5 wells of a Matrigel coated 12-well plate. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor) D. Cryopreservation of iPSCs. 1. Rinse cells w/ PBS, add 300 l Accutase, and incubate for 5 min at 37oC. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. 2. Spin down the cells, resuspend w/ 1ml mFreSR medium (Stemcell, # 05855) containing 10 uM Rock inhibitor. 3. Transfer 1 ml cells to cryovial. Transfer vial to a slow freezing container and store at -80oC overnight, then transfer the frozen tubes to liquid nitrogen tank for long term storage. Phase 1: Embryoid body formation A. Growth of iPSCs to not more than 50% confluence on a Matrigel-coated P100 TC dish. 1. When iPSCs reach 65–80% confluence in a well of a 12 well plate, detach iPSC with Accutase as above, but pipet cells thoroughly to obtain single cell suspension. 2. Count cells and plate 7 x 10^5 cells onto a Matrigel coated P100 plate (Corning Falcon, # 353003) as described above. 3. Change to mTeSR+ medium w/o Rock inhibitor the next day. 4. About 2 days later the iPSC should not higher than 50% confluent.  Keeping the cells at this density is a critical step for successful iPSM differentiation. B. Formation of embryoid bodies in AggreWell microwell plate 1. Pre-treat one well for each iPS line in a 24-well AggreWell 800 plate (each well has 300 micropits) with 500 L Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution (both contained in Stemcell Technologies, catalog#: 34850). 2. Centrifuge plate at 1300 x g for 5 minutes in a swinging bucket rotor fitted with plate holders. Observe plate under a microscope to ensure that bubbles have been removed from microwells. If bubbles remain trapped in any microwells, centrifuge at 1300 x g for an additional 5 minutes. 3. Aspirate Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution from the wells. Rinse each well with 2 ml warm mTeSR Plus medium. 4. Aspirate medium from the well. Add 1 ml warm EB medium (Table 1) supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor to each well to be used. 5. Rinse 50% confluent iPSC w/ PBS and detach iPSCs with Accutase, as described above, but pipet thoroughly into single cells. Then add mTeSR Plus to quench Accutase, as above. 6. Count cells, spin down 4.5 x 10^6 IPSCs at 250 x g for 5 min. Resuspend pellet in 1 ml of EB medium supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor (10 M). 7. Add cells to well to yield achieve a final volume of: 2 mL. 8. Pipette cells up and down gently several times with P1000 pipettor to ensure even distribution. Spin plate at 100 x g for 3min. Under microscope, piled iPSCs should be observed in each microwell (Figures 1, 2). 9. Incubate the plate at 37°C in 5% O2 5% CO2 incubator. Feed with 1 ml EB medium (w/o rock inhibitor) every day for 7 days. Compact EB should be observed by day 2 (Figure 2). Phase 2: Generation of macrophage progenitors. A. Add 10 ml macrophage progenitor culture medium (Table 2) to gelatin-coated (4 ml of 0.1% gelatin incubated at 37oC for 1 hr, MP, catalog number: 901771) P100 tissue culture dish. B. Remove and discard 0.5 ml media from each well of the EB culture, yielding 1.5 ml remaining. Cut a P1000 pipet tip with sterile blade to widen the opening. Gently suspend EBs with P1000 pipet tip to dislocate each EB from the microwells. C. Transfer free EBs to a 1.5 ml sterile microfuge tube. EBs will spontaneously sediment in 1 min. Discard the supernatant and use wide open P1000 pipet tip to resuspend the EBs with progenitor culture medium.  D. Transfer resuspended EBs into previously prepared P100 tissue culture dish mentioned in step A. E. Move the P100 dish back and forth and side to side, without rotating, to evenly distribute EBs and incubate in hypoxic incubator for the first 7 days of phase 2, then move to regular incubator. Replenish with progenitor culture medium every 4 days. EBs will attach to the P100 dish and began to grow out. Harvesting macrophage progenitors. Over time, macrophage progenitors are released from the EBs and float in medium. Starting on day 29, floating progenitors in 10 ml medium may be harvested every 4 days, which are then passed through a sterile 50 m cell filter (Sysmex, #04-004-2326), to remove EB fragments, and plated onto a new gelatin coated P100 tissue culture plate. The phase 2 EBs are replenished with 10 ml progenitor culture medium. Phase 3: iPSMs maturation and maintenance Macrophage progenitors attach to the gelatin coated plates overnight and the medium is replaced with macrophage maintenance medium (Table 3).  Replenish cells with fresh maintenance medium every 4 days until usage (usually 7-10 days after harvesting progenitors).

Transfer 1 ml of cells each into two wells of the Matrigel coated 12 well plate. Place into hypoxic (5% O2) incubator. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor). C. Passage of iPSCs by the clump culture method. 1. Ideally, cells should be growing in small clumps and reached 65–80% confluence before passage. Split at 1:3 to 1:5 every 3–4 days to avoid confluence which can lead to spontaneous loss of pluripotency. 2. Rinse cells w/ PBS, add 300 l Accutase (Gibco, # A1110501), and incubate for 5 min at 37oC. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. Dilute cells as needed in mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor and plate cells into 3-5 wells of a Matrigel coated 12-well plate. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor) D. Cryopreservation of iPSCs. 1. Rinse cells w/ PBS, add 300 l Accutase, and incubate for 5 min at 37oC. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. 2. Spin down the cells, resuspend w/ 1ml mFreSR medium (Stemcell, # 05855) containing 10 uM Rock inhibitor. 3. Transfer 1 ml cells to cryovial. Transfer vial to a slow freezing container and store at -80oC overnight, then transfer the frozen tubes to liquid nitrogen tank for long term storage. Phase 1: Embryoid body formation A. Growth of iPSCs to not more than 50% confluence on a Matrigel-coated P100 TC dish. 1. When iPSCs reach 65–80% confluence in a well of a 12 well plate, detach iPSC with Accutase as above, but pipet cells thoroughly to obtain single cell suspension. 2. Count cells and plate 7 x 10^5 cells onto a Matrigel coated P100 plate (Corning Falcon, # 353003) as described above. 3. Change to mTeSR+ medium w/o Rock inhibitor the next day. 4. About 2 days later the iPSC should not higher than 50% confluent.  Keeping the cells at this density is a critical step for successful iPSM differentiation. B. Formation of embryoid bodies in AggreWell microwell plate 1. Pre-treat one well for each iPS line in a 24-well AggreWell 800 plate (each well has 300 micropits) with 500 L Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution (both contained in Stemcell Technologies, catalog#: 34850). 2. Centrifuge plate at 1300 x g for 5 minutes in a swinging bucket rotor fitted with plate holders. Observe plate under a microscope to ensure that bubbles have been removed from microwells. If bubbles remain trapped in any microwells, centrifuge at 1300 x g for an additional 5 minutes. 3. Aspirate Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution from the wells. Rinse each well with 2 ml warm mTeSR Plus medium. 4. Aspirate medium from the well. Add 1 ml warm EB medium (Table 1) supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor to each well to be used. 5. Rinse 50% confluent iPSC w/ PBS and detach iPSCs with Accutase, as described above, but pipet thoroughly into single cells. Then add mTeSR Plus to quench Accutase, as above. 6. Count cells, spin down 4.5 x 10^6 IPSCs at 250 x g for 5 min. Resuspend pellet in 1 ml of EB medium supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor (10 M). 7. Add cells to well to yield achieve a final volume of: 2 mL. 8. Pipette cells up and down gently several times with P1000 pipettor to ensure even distribution. Spin plate at 100 x g for 3min. Under microscope, piled iPSCs should be observed in each microwell (Figures 1, 2). 9. Incubate the plate at 37°C in 5% O2 5% CO2 incubator. Feed with 1 ml EB medium (w/o rock inhibitor) every day for 7 days. Compact EB should be observed by day 2 (Figure 2). Phase 2: Generation of macrophage progenitors. A. Add 10 ml macrophage progenitor culture medium (Table 2) to gelatin-coated (4 ml of 0.1% gelatin incubated at 37oC for 1 hr, MP, catalog number: 901771) P100 tissue culture dish. B. Remove and discard 0.5 ml media from each well of the EB culture, yielding 1.5 ml remaining. Cut a P1000 pipet tip with sterile blade to widen the opening. Gently suspend EBs with P1000 pipet tip to dislocate each EB from the microwells. C. Transfer free EBs to a 1.5 ml sterile microfuge tube. EBs will spontaneously sediment in 1 min. Discard the supernatant and use wide open P1000 pipet tip to resuspend the EBs with progenitor culture medium.  D. Transfer resuspended EBs into previously prepared P100 tissue culture dish mentioned in step A. E. Move the P100 dish back and forth and side to side, without rotating, to evenly distribute EBs and incubate in hypoxic incubator for the first 7 days of phase 2, then move to regular incubator. Replenish with progenitor culture medium every 4 days. EBs will attach to the P100 dish and began to grow out. Harvesting macrophage progenitors. Over time, macrophage progenitors are released from the EBs and float in medium. Starting on day 29, floating progenitors in 10 ml medium may be harvested every 4 days, which are then passed through a sterile 50 m cell filter (Sysmex, #04-004-2326), to remove EB fragments, and plated onto a new gelatin coated P100 tissue culture plate. The phase 2 EBs are replenished with 10 ml progenitor culture medium. Phase 3: iPSMs maturation and maintenance Macrophage progenitors attach to the gelatin coated plates overnight and the medium is replaced with macrophage maintenance medium (Table 3).  Replenish cells with fresh maintenance medium every 4 days until usage (usually 7-10 days after harvesting progenitors).

Count cells and plate 7 x 10^5 cells onto a Matrigel coated P100 plate (Corning Falcon, # 353003) as described above. 3. Change to mTeSR+ medium w/o Rock inhibitor the next day. 4. About 2 days later the iPSC should not higher than 50% confluent.  Keeping the cells at this density is a critical step for successful iPSM differentiation. B. Formation of embryoid bodies in AggreWell microwell plate 1. Pre-treat one well for each iPS line in a 24-well AggreWell 800 plate (each well has 300 micropits) with 500 L Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution (both contained in Stemcell Technologies, catalog#: 34850). 2. Centrifuge plate at 1300 x g for 5 minutes in a swinging bucket rotor fitted with plate holders. Observe plate under a microscope to ensure that bubbles have been removed from microwells. If bubbles remain trapped in any microwells, centrifuge at 1300 x g for an additional 5 minutes. 3. Aspirate Anti-Adherence Rinsing Solution from the wells. Rinse each well with 2 ml warm mTeSR Plus medium. 4. Aspirate medium from the well. Add 1 ml warm EB medium (Table 1) supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor to each well to be used. 5. Rinse 50% confluent iPSC w/ PBS and detach iPSCs with Accutase, as described above, but pipet thoroughly into single cells. Then add mTeSR Plus to quench Accutase, as above. 6. Count cells, spin down 4.5 x 10^6 IPSCs at 250 x g for 5 min. Resuspend pellet in 1 ml of EB medium supplemented w/ Rock inhibitor (10 M). 7. Add cells to well to yield achieve a final volume of: 2 mL. 8. Pipette cells up and down gently several times with P1000 pipettor to ensure even distribution. Spin plate at 100 x g for 3min. Under microscope, piled iPSCs should be observed in each microwell (Figures 1, 2). 9. Incubate the plate at 37°C in 5% O2 5% CO2 incubator. Feed with 1 ml EB medium (w/o rock inhibitor) every day for 7 days. Compact EB should be observed by day 2 (Figure 2). Phase 2: Generation of macrophage progenitors. A. Add 10 ml macrophage progenitor culture medium (Table 2) to gelatin-coated (4 ml of 0.1% gelatin incubated at 37oC for 1 hr, MP, catalog number: 901771) P100 tissue culture dish. B. Remove and discard 0.5 ml media from each well of the EB culture, yielding 1.5 ml remaining. Cut a P1000 pipet tip with sterile blade to widen the opening. Gently suspend EBs with P1000 pipet tip to dislocate each EB from the microwells. C. Transfer free EBs to a 1.5 ml sterile microfuge tube. EBs will spontaneously sediment in 1 min. Discard the supernatant and use wide open P1000 pipet tip to resuspend the EBs with progenitor culture medium.  D. Transfer resuspended EBs into previously prepared P100 tissue culture dish mentioned in step A. E. Move the P100 dish back and forth and side to side, without rotating, to evenly distribute EBs and incubate in hypoxic incubator for the first 7 days of phase 2, then move to regular incubator. Replenish with progenitor culture medium every 4 days. EBs will attach to the P100 dish and began to grow out. Harvesting macrophage progenitors. Over time, macrophage progenitors are released from the EBs and float in medium. Starting on day 29, floating progenitors in 10 ml medium may be harvested every 4 days, which are then passed through a sterile 50 m cell filter (Sysmex, #04-004-2326), to remove EB fragments, and plated onto a new gelatin coated P100 tissue culture plate. The phase 2 EBs are replenished with 10 ml progenitor culture medium. Phase 3: iPSMs maturation and maintenance Macrophage progenitors attach to the gelatin coated plates overnight and the medium is replaced with macrophage maintenance medium (Table 3).  Replenish cells with fresh maintenance medium every 4 days until usage (usually 7-10 days after harvesting progenitors).

Thawing and initial plating of iPSCs. 1. iPSCs were generated by the California Institute of Regenerative Medicine and purchased from the Coriell Institute (now managed by FUJIFILM Cellular Dynamics). 2. Thaw frozen iPS vial in 37°C water bath until just thawed. Add thawed cells to 5 mL mTeSR Plus medium (Stemcell technologies, Catalog # 100-0276) in a 15 ml conical tube (Falcon corning, # 352097). Spin and wash cells with ~1 mL of mTeSR Plus medium. Spin cells and resuspend in 2 ml of mTeSR Plus medium containing 10 uM Y2763 ROCK inhibitor (Tocris, #1254). Passage of iPSCs by the clump culture method. 1. Ideally, cells should be growing in small clumps and reached 65–80% confluence before passage. Split at 1:3 to 1:5 every 3–4 days to avoid confluence which can lead to spontaneous loss of pluripotency. 2. Dilute cells as needed in mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor and plate cells into 3-5 wells of a Matrigel coated 12-well plate. 3. Change media every day with 1 ml mTeSR+ medium (w/o Rock inhibitor) D. With a P1000 tip, add 1 mL of mTeSR+ w/ROCK inhibitor, and gently pipet up and down to lift cells from the plate in small clumps without dissociating into single cells. 2. Spin down the cells, resuspend w/ 1ml mFreSR medium (Stemcell, # 05855) containing 10 uM Rock inhibitor. 3. Transfer 1 ml cells to cryovial.
