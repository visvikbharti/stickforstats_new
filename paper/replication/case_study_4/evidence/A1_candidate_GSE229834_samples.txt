!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = The obtained RNA was fragmented using a fragmentation buffer, and reverse transcription was performed with random N6 primers to synthesize cDNA double strands. The synthesized double-stranded DNA was end-repaired and 5'-phosphorylated, and the 3'-end was extended with an "A" tail to form a sticky end. A bubble-shaped adapter with a protruding "T" at the 3' end was then ligated to the DNA fragments, which were PCR amplified using specific primers. The PCR products were denatured to form single-stranded DNA, which was circularized using a bridge primer to obtain a single-stranded circular DNA library.
