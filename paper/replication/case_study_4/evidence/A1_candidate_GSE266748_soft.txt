
1. GATA6 is a Novel Regulator of Gene Expression and 3D Genome in Colorectal Cancer (RNA-Seq)
(Submitter supplied) Colorectal cancer (CRC) is one of the most lethal and prevalent malignancies with elusive molecular causes. While the abnormal activity of transcription factors has been reported as a vital component of cancer progression, the mechanism through which they function remains unclear. Here, through the analysis of primary human CRC tissue data, we identified GATA6 as a CRC specific regulator, whose expression upregulation is potentially associated with promoter hypomethylation. more...
