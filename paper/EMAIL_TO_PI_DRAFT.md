### Paper Summary

**Title:** StickForStats: A Statistical Analysis Platform with Automatic Assumption Validation

**Key Innovation:** Unlike existing statistical software where assumption testing is optional, StickForStats implements **mandatory assumption validation** through the Guardian system. Every statistical test automatically validates its assumptions before execution, preventing the common problem of researchers unknowingly violating statistical assumptions.

### Paper Highlights

The manuscript (37 pages) covers:

1. **Guardian System** - Automatic assumption validation with 8 validators covering normality, variance homogeneity, independence, outliers, sample size, modality, linearity, and homoscedasticity

2. **AI Statistical Advisor** - Claude-powered natural language guidance for test selection and interpretation

3. **Paper Parser** - Manuscript analysis for JARS-Quant compliance with a prototype Statistical Quality Score (SQS) system

4. **High-Precision Computing** - 50-decimal precision for exact p-value calculations using mpmath

5. **Comprehensive Validation** - Benchmarked against R, SciPy, and statsmodels with documented accuracy
