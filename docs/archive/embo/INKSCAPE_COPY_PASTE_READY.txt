====== SUMMARY BOX (Top section, below title) ======

THE CHALLENGE
70% of researchers fail to reproduce published experiments¹, with 11-38% of papers containing statistical errors² that lead to retractions, wasted resources, and irreproducible findings. Cost: $327.6M spacecraft losses³, billions in failed drug development, and diminished public trust in science.

OUR SOLUTION
StickForStats Guardian is the first platform that actively BLOCKS invalid statistical tests before execution. Unlike conventional tools that merely calculate, Guardian validates assumptions (normality, variance homogeneity, linearity) in <200ms and prevents Type I errors at the source.

IMPACT
77.3% validation coverage across 22 statistical tests, 32 interactive educational lessons, zero cost for academia. Guardian transforms statistical analysis from error-prone calculations into a guided, reproducible workflow.

VISION
Make rigorous statistics accessible and reproducible science inevitable, not optional.
