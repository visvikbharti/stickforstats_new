📊 Retraction Panel Layout Comparison

Compare both layout options for your EMBO poster's "Statistical Failures" section

⚡ Both layouts are scientifically accurate, citation-safe, and ready to use

OPTION 1: Compact Table
Information-dense, professional, organized

✅ Strengths

  • Maximum information density - Fits all cases with details
  • Easy to read - Clear categories, logical flow
  • Professional appearance - Academic conference standard
  • Space-efficient - Fits in smaller poster area if needed
  • Good for text-focused visitors - Researchers who read details

⚠️ Considerations

  • More text-heavy (may not catch eye from distance)
  • Requires closer reading to appreciate impact
  • Less visually striking than cards
OPTION 2: Visual Cards
Visually striking, memorable, scannable

✅ Strengths

  • Visually impactful - Catches attention from across room
  • Memorable layout - 9-grid format sticks in mind
  • Quick scanning - Numbers pop out immediately
  • Guardian card stands out - Clear solution vs. problems
  • Modern design - Contemporary poster aesthetic

⚠️ Considerations

  • Requires more poster space (3×3 grid)
  • Less detailed explanations in each card
  • Need to explain details verbally to visitors
