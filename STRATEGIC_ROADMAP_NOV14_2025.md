**Probability**: Medium (30-40% for top journals)
**Impact**: Medium (delays publication, not fatal)

**Mitigation**:
- Choose appropriate journal tier (aim for IF: 3-11)
- Get feedback from PI/colleagues before submission
- Prepare for revision (incorporate reviewer feedback)
- Have backup journals ready (submit to 2nd choice if rejected)

**Fallback**:
- Conference papers (easier acceptance)
- Preprint on arXiv (immediate visibility)
- Tech blog series (broader audience)

**Impact**: Medium (product growth slows, not research)

### What "100% Authenticity" Means Going Forward

**In Publications**:
- ✅ Report pilot study honestly (n=12, preliminary findings)
- ✅ Acknowledge limitations (small sample, no control group)
- ✅ Frame as "proof of concept" not "definitive evidence"
- ✅ Cite all sources properly (Baker 2016, component audits)
- ✅ Never fabricate data, case studies, or testimonials

**In Product Marketing**:
- ✅ Claim only what can be demonstrated (77.3% coverage, <200ms)
- ✅ Show real screenshots, real data
- ✅ User testimonials only from real users (with permission)
- ✅ Comparison to competitors only if formally tested
- ✅ Transparent about limitations (beta, PostgreSQL migration, etc.)

**In Technical Documentation**:
- ✅ Document all design decisions with rationale
- ✅ Acknowledge technical debt and plans to address
- ✅ Report bugs honestly, track resolution
- ✅ Provide evidence for performance claims (testing reports)

**In User Studies**:
- ✅ IRB approval (if required)
- ✅ Informed consent from all participants
- ✅ Anonymize data before publication
- ✅ Share negative findings (what didn't work)
- ✅ Pre-register study protocols (Open Science Framework)
